An input is named <region>_<unit> (e.g. ITS1_asv). OTUs are the 97 % clusters of the ASVs; the datasets that publish OTUs are not re-clustered (OD 30c). full = ITS1 + 5.8S + ITS2.

What goes into one query input

real unitsITSx on the ASVs or OTUsMiscMetabar::itsx_pq() keeps the region of the input; its putative origin is kept in ITSx_origin (information only, SA7).
+
fake_ controlsShuffled copies of the trimmed real unitsprop_fake = 0.5: half as many as the real units (Pauvert ASV 97, OTU 75; wood 437). Seeded. Correct answer: NA at every rank.
+
external_ controls100 non-fungal records, trimmed by ITSxDrawn from Unite_s_all_20250219 in the kingdoms every _Fungi_rep database keeps; the same 100 records in every input (identical trimmed ones merged). Truth = their UNITE lineage, placeholders set to NA (SA4, D).

Mocks are scored per unit and per rank (Kingdom → Species) with F1, PPV, TPR, MCC. Labels such as *_Incertae_sedis or unclassified count as NA on both sides. Biological datasets have no truth and are read through their NA and control rates only (decision 26).
